Cozumel · Cultural · Hands-on

Honey Traditions of Cozumel with Hands-On Chocolate & Salsa

On this tour, guests head into a private jungle preserve to meet the sacred Melipona bees — the stingless bee the Maya considered divine — and taste their honey straight from the comb. Then it's all hands-on: they grind cacao into their own Mayan chocolate, make fresh salsa, and press their own tortillas. They finish by tasting everything they made, molding a beeswax candle to take home, and sampling artisanal jungle liqueurs. Small group, about four hours.

Itinerary · ~4 hours
  1. Tierra Maya Jungle Sanctuary — a private cultural preserve
  2. Melipona Honey Tasting — raw honeycomb, guided
  3. Ceremonial Cacao — make your own chocolate
  4. Zik'il P'aak & Tortillas — grind & press by hand
  5. Beeswax Candle & Marketplace — a candle to take home
  6. Jungle Liqueur Tasting — the finale
Great for guests who say: “something authentic,” “we love food & culture,” “not another beach,” or “something the kids will remember.”

Jungle sanctuary
Tierra Maya Jungle Sanctuary

Arrive at a hidden jungle sanctuary — a private cultural preserve protecting the sacred stingless Melipona bees and the Mayan traditions of honey, medicine and the land.

“You're leaving the tourist trail — most visitors never find this.”

Honey tasting
Melipona Honey Tasting

A guided honey tasting of varieties reflecting Cozumel's flora — hold raw honeycomb and learn why traditional harvesting keeps the colonies alive.

“The sacred bee only 5% of visitors ever encounter.”

Grinding cacao
Ceremonial Cacao

Grind cacao beans on a volcanic-stone molcajete, then blend with honey, chili or cinnamon to craft your own Mayan chocolate drink.

“Hands-on, not a demo — they make it themselves.”

Salsa & tortillas
Zik'il P'aak & Tortillas

Prepare Zik'il P'aak, a Mayan pumpkin-seed salsa with fire-roasted tomatoes — grind by hand and press fresh tortillas with traditional tools, served with handmade chips.

“A full hands-on Mayan kitchen.”

Beeswax candle
Beeswax Candle & Marketplace

Mold your own natural beeswax candle to take home, then browse the farm's marketplace — Melipona honey, artisan salsas and handcrafted goods that fund conservation.

“They leave with something they made.”

Liqueur tasting
Jungle Liqueur Tasting

Conclude the journey with tastings of artisanal jungle liqueurs.

“A relaxed finish for the grown-ups.”
