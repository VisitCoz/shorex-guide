Cozumel · Market-to-Table · Hands-on

The Native Kitchen: Local Market and Cooking Journey

On this tour, guests shop a real Cozumel neighborhood market with their guide — hand-picking fresh ingredients at a street tortillería and the municipal market — then cross the island to a jungle sanctuary to cook it all from scratch. They grind corn, press tortillas, and make their own salsa and guacamole with ancient tools over fire, then sit down to a jungle-to-table lunch of what they made. It's fully hands-on, and naturally gluten-free and vegan-friendly. About four and a half hours.

Itinerary · ~4.5 hours
  1. Scenic start — Malecón drive + photo stop
  2. Street tortillería — buy fresh tortillas
  3. Shop the market — hand-pick ingredients
  4. Cross the island — East Coast / Mezcalitos
  5. Cook it yourself — metate, molcajete & comal
  6. Jungle-to-table lunch — eat what you made
Great for guests who say: “we love food,” “a real cooking class,” “something local & authentic,” or “we have dietary restrictions.”

Malecón drive
Scenic start

A panoramic waterfront drive along the Malecón, with a photo stop at the island monuments and the WWII “Aztec Eagles” airplane.

“See the island before you taste it.”

Tortillería
Street tortillería

Join locals in line at a bustling street-side tortillería, buy a kilo of fresh tortillas, and feel the heat straight off the machine.

“An authentic, unfiltered Mexican moment.”

Municipal market
Shop the market

Hand-pick your own ingredients — avocado, tomato, cilantro, pumpkin seeds — as your guide explains island life: supplies arrive by boat, so you cook what's fresh today.

“Shop like a local, not a tourist.”

The Wild Side
Cross the island

To the uninhabited East Coast, where jungle meets open sea, with a photo stop at the iconic Mezcalitos lookout.

“The Cozumel most cruisers never see.”

Cook at Tierra Maya
Cook it yourself

Master three ancient tools at the jungle sanctuary — grind corn on the metate, crush salsa in the molcajete, cook tortillas on the wood-fired comal.

“Hands-on the whole time — a real cooking class.”

Jungle-to-table lunch
Jungle-to-table lunch

Enjoy what you made — handmade quesadillas and guacamole on clay plates, a cacao dessert, and a souvenir to take home.

“They eat everything they made.”

Notes for selling this tour▶
What guests love most

Shopping a real market and then cooking it over fire — it's fully hands-on, start to finish. And because guests make their own tortillas and guacamole, it's naturally gluten-free, vegetarian and vegan-friendly.

A great fit for

Foodies · curious travelers who want the real island · guests with dietary needs · couples and families wanting something immersive.

Good to know

About 4.5 hours with moderate walking, including a busy market. Air-conditioned transport between stops; the cooking is in an open-air jungle venue (light clothing + insect repellent recommended). Not wheelchair accessible.

Handy if a guest asks…

“Is it worth it?” — It's a full market tour and a hands-on cooking class with lunch — you shop it, cook it, and eat it.

“I have a food allergy / I'm vegan.” — Great fit — the food is made fresh by hand; just tell the guide on arrival (including nut/pumpkin-seed allergies).

“Much walking?” — Moderate, mostly around the market and sanctuary; A/C van in between.
